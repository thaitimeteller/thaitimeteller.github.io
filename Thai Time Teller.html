Thai Time Teller

กี่โมงแล้ว?

Thai Time Teller · Lesson 6 · for beginners

Goals: tell the time, ask the time, and talk about your day in Thai.

Ask the time

  • กี่โมง kee mong · any time
  • กี่ทุ่ม kee tum · 7–11 p.m.
  • ตีอะไร tee a-rai · 1–5 a.m.
  • ครึ่ง krueng = half past · ตรง trong = sharp

Why ตี, โมง, ทุ่ม?

Old Siam told time with sound: a gong by day (“moong”), a drum in the evening (“thum”), and a strike of the gong late at night (ตี).
